## Patterns of the Top 10%

These 5 patterns separate elite Argentine software sites from average ones:

### 1. Specific Outcome Language (not features)
Winners say: "Automatizá la lectura de facturas en 3 segundos" — not "ofrecemos integración de sistemas."
Every top site leads with a specific business outcome. Atika's current copy is process-oriented, not outcome-oriented.

### 2. AI Is Front and Center (even when it's basic)
Finnegans mentions "Finni AI" in the hero. Globant leads with "Enterprise AI." Even companies with simple automation features brand it as AI loudly. Atika's actual OCR/Vision AI — which is genuinely sophisticated — is completely invisible.

### 3. Trust Stack: Certification + Clients + Numbers
Top sites layer: AFIP badge → client logos → hard numbers (60,000 empresas, 35 años). The stack removes risk for prospects. Atika has 22 client logos but no numbers and no certifications visible.

### 4. WhatsApp as Primary Support CTA (Argentina-specific)
Thomson Reuters, Tango — both use WhatsApp prominently because Argentine B2B buyers expect it. No email forms only.

### 5. Demo/Free Trial or Immediate Value CTA
Finnegans: "Quiero usar Finnegans." Tango: direct product access. The best sites don't just say "contactanos" — they remove friction to first experience.

## Recommended Design Direction for Atika

Based on competitor analysis:

**Colors:**
- Deep Navy `#0F1729` (trust, technology, legacy expertise)
- Electric Blue `#2563EB` (AI, innovation, modernity)
- Warm White `#F8F9FA` (clarity, readability)
- Gold Accent `#F59E0B` (premium, financial sector alignment)

**Typography:**
- Headings: `Inter` or `Plus Jakarta Sans` — modern tech, readable
- Body: `Inter` — consistent, high legibility at all sizes

**Design Patterns:**
- Dark hero section with glowing blue accents (matches AI/tech category leaders)
- Document/OCR animation in hero (visual metaphor for core service)
- Trust stack: AFIP logo + client logos + years in market
- Before/After section: "Before Atika: manual entry" → "After: instant DB sync"
- WhatsApp floating CTA (mandatory for Argentine market)
